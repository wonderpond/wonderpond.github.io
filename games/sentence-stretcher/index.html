Sentence stretcher

"The frog jumped." is a sentence, but it is a little bit boring. Stretch it by answering what kind, where, and when!

Each sentence gets three details. What kind: a describing word that tells more about who. Where: a place. When: a time. Pick the detail that answers each question, and watch your sentence grow. Grades 2 and 3.
