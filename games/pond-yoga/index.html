Pond yoga

Stretch like a frog, balance like a heron, and breathe like the calm pond. A yoga break for the whole class.

How long?

The coach reads each pose aloud, so turn the sound on. Each pose is held for about 20 seconds with slow breaths, and there is a short calm breath between poses. Move gently and only stretch as far as feels good. Grades K to 5.
