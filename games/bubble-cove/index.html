Bubble Cove

Trap sea critters in bubbles, pop them, and clear the cove.

Controls

1P: ← → move, ↑ or Z jump, Space or X blow a bubble

2P: A D move, W jump, F blow a bubble

P pause, M sound. If the keys do nothing, click the game screen once.

How to play

Blow a bubble to trap an enemy, then touch the bubble to pop it and it drops a treat. Bubbles that touch pop together, and every extra enemy in a chain doubles the points.

Leave a trapped enemy too long and it breaks free, red and faster. Hold jump as you land on a bubble to bounce off it like a trampoline.

Classroom mode

Pick a subject and a grade band above. During each round a question appears under the score bar and three answer bubbles drift down from the top of the cave, so they will not bump into you on their way in. Pop the correct one for 2,000 points, a pearl, and eight seconds of rapid bubbles.

A wrong pop costs 1,000 points, so it pays to read before you jump. Whether you answer or the bubbles drift away, the correct answer is shown afterward. Up to four questions per round, drawn from a bank of 177 items. Choose arcade only to turn questions off.

King battles

Every fifth round a crowned king appears. One bubble is not enough: keep hitting it and watch the pink meter over its head. When the meter empties the king is sealed in a giant bubble, so touch it fast before it breaks free with half its meter back.

Each king is tougher than the last. More hits to seal, faster charges, more minions, a shorter bubble timer, and from the second king on it spits ink you have to dodge. Sealing one clears every minion on screen and pays 10,000 points and up.

Secrets

Collect the letter bubbles B, O, N, U, S to earn an extra life. Take too long and an ink shade that drifts through walls comes after you. Drop through the gap in the floor and you reappear at the top.
